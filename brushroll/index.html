Brushroll

Maze Paint Puzzle

Swipe to send the ball rolling. It slides until it hits a wall and paints every tile it passes. Fill the whole maze with color to clear the level.

Plays offlineEndless levelsDaily challenge

How to play

  1. Swipe up, down, left or right.
  2. The ball rolls until it hits a wall and paints every tile it passes.
  3. Color every tile to clear the level. Finish within par for a PERFECT bonus.

Why you'll love it

  • Endless maze levels that get trickier as you go, every one checked to be solvable
  • A new Daily Challenge maze every day, with a streak bonus
  • One-finger swipe controls, perfect for quick breaks
  • Stuck? A hint shows your next 3 moves
  • Earn stars to unlock 15 color themes and ball skins
  • Plays offline, anywhere: no Wi-Fi needed

Free to play with ads and optional in-app purchases. The leaderboard is a practice league with simulated rivals.
