Brushroll Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Brushroll ("the app") is a single-player puzzle game. This policy explains what information is handled when you play.

Information the app stores

Your game progress (level, score, stars, unlocked themes and balls, the name you choose for the leaderboard, and settings) is stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. Google may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP address, and app interaction data to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In regions where it is required, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices on the home screen.

Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. The app never sees or stores your payment details; it only receives confirmation of what you bought so it can unlock it.

Leaderboard and sharing

The current leaderboard is computed on your device and includes simulated rival players. Your name and score are not uploaded. When you tap Share, the app opens Android's share sheet with a message you can review; nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
